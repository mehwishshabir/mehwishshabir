
# Mehwish Shabir

**Bioinformatics Researcher | Biomedical Data Analysis | AI in Healthcare**

I am a bioinformatics researcher based in Islamabad, Pakistan, with an MS in Bioinformatics from the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST).
My work focuses on applying machine learning and deep learning to clinical data, particularly medical imaging and cardiovascular signals. I am currently working on the PakHeart research initiative at NUST, where I contribute to ECG dataset development, signal-quality assessment, clinical annotation and AI validation.
Previously, my research focused on automated intracranial hemorrhage analysis using 3D deep-learning models and evaluating how well these models generalise across different clinical imaging datasets.

## Research Experience

### PakHeart | Cardiovascular AI and ECG Data
**Research Assistant, SEECS–NUST | August 2026 – Present**

PakHeart is a research initiative developing ECG data infrastructure for cardiovascular research in Pakistan.
My work includes:
- ECG data ingestion and waveform-format assessment
- Signal-quality evaluation and clinical metadata validation
- Development of an ECG diagnostic taxonomy covering 8 superclasses and 38 subclasses
- Cardiologist-guided annotation and data quality-control workflows
- Preliminary research on ECG lead reduction and reconstruction

[PakHeart GitHub Organisation](https://github.com/PakHeart)

### Intracranial Hemorrhage Analysis | MS Research
**Graduate Researcher, SINES–NUST | 2024–2025**
For my MS research, I developed and evaluated deep-learning methods for intracranial hemorrhage analysis using brain CT images.
- Implemented 3D CNN, U-Net and V-Net architectures for classification and segmentation
- Worked with clinical imaging datasets and radiologist-reviewed annotations
- Investigated cross-dataset generalisation and image-intensity variation
- Improved external classification accuracy from 64% to 93% following intensity normalisation
- Developed HemoViz, a research prototype for hemorrhage visualisation and volumetric analysis

### Ischemic Stroke AI Research
I have also contributed to research involving AI-based ischemic stroke classification and lesion segmentation using clinical CT imaging datasets.
My contributions included data preprocessing, annotation review, model evaluation and research documentation.
