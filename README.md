## Research Philosophy

I am interested in computational research that goes beyond predictive performance.

For healthcare AI, strong accuracy is important, but so are:

**reproducibility • interpretability • data quality • clinical relevance • traceability**

These principles guide my interest in both AI model development and the research infrastructure required to support reliable biomedical AI.
